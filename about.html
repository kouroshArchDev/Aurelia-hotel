About Aurelia

A place shaped by
how you want to feel.

A private estate in Ubud created around quiet luxury, natural beauty, and the simple pleasure of having nowhere else to be.

The Aurelia Story

Luxury, with
a softer voice.

Aurelia was imagined as a retreat rather than a spectacle. The architecture follows the landscape, interiors are tactile and calm, and service is designed to feel intuitive rather than intrusive.

Every room, meal, garden path, and experience is part of the same idea: give people the space to slow down and notice where they are.
